# Arc Microgrants candidate groups

Research date: 2026-10-08.

Classification of 112 candidate project repositories. Each repository is assigned to one primary product function, based on its README. This is an analyst classification, not official tracks or participant counts. Hybrid projects can fit other categories. Counts include planned, prototype and deployed projects; mainnet and submission claims are not independently verified.

| Primary group | Count |
|---|---|
| Payment links, invoices, tips | 17 |
| Escrow, milestone work, conditional funding | 12 |
| Splits, group settlement, streams, scheduled payouts | 11 |
| Agent budgets, policy wallets, treasury execution | 14 |
| Receipts, provenance, analytics, transaction risk | 21 |
| Paywalls and paid APIs/MCP | 5 |
| Savings, lending, yield, receivables finance | 9 |
| Prediction markets, betting, token markets | 6 |
| Marketplaces, paid work and specialized commerce | 11 |
| Wallet, bridge, workflow, governance infrastructure | 4 |
